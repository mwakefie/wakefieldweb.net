App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy applies to each app developed by Matthew Wakefield that links to this page as its privacy policy (each, "the App"). These apps are designed to be completely private. This policy explains what that means.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal details, and the developer has no access to how you use the App.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any content or preferences you create in the App are stored only on your device. This data is never sent to the developer or any third party. Deleting the App removes this data from your device.

No Advertising, Tracking, or Analytics

The App does not display ads, does not use analytics or tracking services, and does not include any third-party SDKs that collect data. The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

No In-App Purchases

The App does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions. Any purchase of the App itself is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive or store any payment details.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer does not collect any user data, there is no data to retain, share, or delete on our side. You can remove all App data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
